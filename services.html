Patient FAQ

Common questions

Is root canal treatment painful?+

Modern local anesthesia keeps the procedure itself painless. Most patients feel mild soreness for a day or two afterward, managed easily with over-the-counter pain relief.

Do you offer EMI / installment options?+

Yes — for implants, aligners, and larger treatment plans, we offer installment options. Ask our front desk at either branch for current terms.

How often should I get a cleaning?+

We recommend a checkup and cleaning every six months, or more often if you're undergoing active treatment like braces.

Can I switch branches between appointments?+

Yes. Your records are shared across both branches, so you can book whichever location is more convenient each visit.

Do you treat dental emergencies?+

Yes — call our emergency hotline for toothaches, trauma, or broken restorations, and we'll get you seen as soon as possible.
